    Buy and sell zones

    Zone timeframes

    🔔 Price alertsRings once when price reaches your level (Bid, as on the chart). With the robot set up it works with the app closed too.

      The live chart comes straight from TradingView, so the app can't draw on it. Use these zones as levels, or draw them yourself with TradingView's rectangle tool. Switch to Zones chart to see them drawn automatically with the Ask line and bar countdown.

      Auto zones combine order blocks (OB), fair value gaps (FVG), liquidity swings, the previous day's high and low (PDH/PDL), round $10 levels and today's VWAP. Broken or filled zones are removed and untested zones score higher. Each zone is labelled with the timeframe it comes from (M5, H1…); a zone sitting inside a higher-timeframe zone is marked "inside H1" and scores higher. The best two on each side are shown for every timeframe you pick. Each zone has a scalp plan: enter at the zone edge, stop beyond it (0.3 ATR plus your broker's spread, so a spread spike doesn't stop you out early), target the next opposite zone or 2R. AI zones come from your latest AI analysis. Zones against your latest bias are faded.

        How the zone alerts did

        The robot records every zone alert and checks whether price reached the TP or the SL first (if both happen inside one check it counts as a loss; alerts still open after 24 hours expire). Gold's closed hours and the quiet window around big news are skipped.

        Match my broker's price

        A small free robot on Cloudflare checks the price every minute and alerts you before each news release and when price enters a Buy or Sell zone, on Telegram and as notifications from this app, even with the app closed.         Zone alerts use the timeframe and zone choice on the Chart page. The after-news report comes 15 minutes after each release: how many pips gold moved and whether your bias was right (your accuracy updates by itself).

        Add releases to your phone calendar

        Add the upcoming releases to your phone's calendar. Each one rings at the time you chose above and again 1 minute before, even with the app closed and the phone locked.

        iPhone: tap the button, then Add All. Android: open the downloaded file with Samsung Calendar or another calendar app; for Google Calendar, import it at calendar.google.com (Settings, Import & export). Adding again later only updates the same events, it doesn't double them up in iPhone and Samsung calendars.
